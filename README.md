A hooded keeper of shadow and smoke, his eyes full of stars, holds a galaxy in his palm inside a cosmos shattered by glowing fractures. The keeper is small against the vast, broken sky, his face lost in the dark of his hood except for two eyes that contain whole constellations, while the galaxy he cradles glows in his outstretched hand.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
